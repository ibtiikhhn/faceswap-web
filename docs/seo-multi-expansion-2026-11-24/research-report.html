Research findings

  • One photo-focused page can cover multiple face swap, group photo face swap and their practical questions. Separate synonym pages would repeat the same tool and instructions.
  • Google autocomplete surfaced selecting one person, couple photos, mobile apps and access without signup. These are useful sections, not evidence of unlimited free use.
  • The product chooses suitable faces automatically. The page now explains that before users spend a trial on a task needing exact manual assignment.

What changed on the page

  • Expanded workflow, three group-photo use cases, preparation guidance and a five-row troubleshooting table.
  • Added an accessible interactive guide explaining which editing goals fit automatic selection.
  • Added 12 FAQs covering one-person selection, separate identities, trial access, phone uploads, formats and unsupported animation.
  • Kept the tool near the top, improved title and description, and added contextual links to photo swapping, realism, memes and relevant tutorials.
  • No fabricated before/after examples or invented image descriptions were added.

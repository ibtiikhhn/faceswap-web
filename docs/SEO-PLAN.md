## Page ownership and changes

| Page | Intent | Placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | AI photo face swap online; recreate a photo with your face | Distinct title/description, existing hero, short how-to section, real examples, quality advice and visible FAQs |
| `/face-swap` | Swap faces between two photos; online photo editor | Task-focused H1 and intro, upload workflow, phone/formats guidance |
| `/pricing` | Face swap plans and credits | Accurate weekly/monthly plans and standalone credit packs |
| `/blog` | Detailed how-to and troubleshooting | Future CMS guides with original demonstrations |

Do not create separate landing pages for every word-order variation. The homepage briefly answers the how-to; a future guide should provide screenshots, worked examples and troubleshooting rather than repeat homepage copy. Link that guide from the homepage once published, and link its practical steps back to the editor.

Implemented FAQs cover putting a face on another picture, swapping without Photoshop, the one free guest swap, realistic results, blurry results, iPhone/Android, photo suitability, target pose/background, unsupported multiple-face/video features and photo privacy. Answers are server-rendered and useful without FAQ schema. Google generally restricts FAQ rich results to authoritative government/health sites; do not promise a rich result.

## Prioritized CMS briefs — not published yet

1. **How to swap a face in another photo: a two-photo guide.** Slug `how-to-swap-a-face-between-two-photos`. Explain source versus target, JPG/PNG/WebP upload, single visible face, actual editor steps, comparison and download. Include original screenshots and a complete consented example. Related phrases: put your face on another picture, swap a face from one photo to another, without Photoshop. Link to editor, pricing and quality guide.
2. **Why does my face swap look blurry or unnatural?** Slug `why-face-swap-results-look-unnatural`. Show tested examples of small faces, mismatched angle/light and obstructions; explain what changing the source or target fixes and what it does not. No unverified “HD” enhancement promise. Demand is qualitative, metrics unverified.
3. **How to get more realistic photo face swaps.** Slug `realistic-photo-face-swap-tips`. Demonstrate angle, lighting, expression and resolution using original comparisons. Distinguish broad photo preparation from the problem-specific troubleshooting guide. “Realistic face swap” is competitive; quality content is not an easy-ranking guarantee.
4. **How to swap faces on iPhone or Android without installing an app.** Slug `face-swap-photos-on-phone`. Test both phone browsers, explain HEIC export to supported formats and saving downloads. No native app/APK claims. Country-specific metrics still unverified.
5. **Is photo face swapping safe?** Slug `face-swap-photo-privacy`. Explain actual upload handling, processing provider, retention, deletion and consent using implementation and current policies. Avoid absolute privacy/security guarantees; review whenever providers change.

Each article needs a real author, accurate review date, descriptive image alt text, an original example and CMS SEO fields. No empty article shells, invented reviews, fake comparisons or auto-generated keyword pages. Do not publish competitors’ names merely to capture their brand traffic.

## Product accuracy

Single-face photos only. One successful guest swap without signup; continued use requires Google sign-in and either a subscription or purchased credits. Credit packs can be used without a subscription. No unlimited-free, group/multiple-face, video, GIF, native-app, offline, passport/ID or sexualized acquisition pages. Explain “realistic” as a quality objective, never “most realistic” without comparative evidence.

## Technical SEO and favicon

The live `/favicon.ico` returned 404 during this audit. Added an App Router favicon ICO, stable 96px PNG icon metadata, 180px Apple icon, 512px brand image and default 1200×630 social preview. These are local changes until deployed. Icons use the existing brand aperture mark. Regenerate with `node --import tsx scripts/generate-brand-assets.ts`.

Preserve distinct canonicals, published-post sitemap filtering, production-only indexing, noindex private pages and truthful WebSite/BlogPosting metadata. Avoid invented ratings or offers. Existing CMS metadata fields remain available.

After deployment:
1. Check `/favicon.ico` and `/favicon-96.png` return 200 with image content types and that homepage HTML links the icon. Keep icon URLs stable and crawlable by Googlebot and Googlebot-Image.
2. Check production homepage/editor canonicals, index/follow, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Verify the alternate hostname redirects to the chosen canonical domain.
3. Submit the sitemap and request homepage indexing in Google Search Console. Google favicon refresh can take days to weeks and display is not guaranteed.
4. Establish a 28-day Search Console baseline by query, page, country and device. Track non-brand impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and completed swaps/purchases. Compare equivalent periods; new sites may need substantially longer to accumulate data.
5. Prioritize new articles from actual relevant impressions and user questions. Recheck country metrics before expanding. Do not change titles weekly or mistake a single snapshot for a rising trend.
